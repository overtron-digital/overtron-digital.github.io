Dual N-Back: Brain Training

Train your working memory with the scientifically-backed dual n-back task.

What is Dual N-Back?

In the dual n-back task, you see positions on a 3×3 grid and hear spoken letters simultaneously. Your goal is to identify when the current stimulus matches the one from N steps back. Research by Jaeggi et al. has shown this exercise can improve working memory and fluid intelligence.

How to Play

Watch & Listen

A square highlights on the grid while a letter is spoken aloud. Both happen at the same time.

Compare

Decide if the current position or letter matches what happened N steps ago.

Respond

Tap “Position” for a visual match, “Audio” for a sound match, or both. Do nothing if there’s no match.
